# Cosmonautics Parts

**Cosmonautics Parts** is a focused, standalone parts edition of **Create: Cosmonautics** built for **Minecraft 1.21.1 (NeoForge)**, **Create**, **Create Aeronautics**, and **Sable**.

All space dimensions, orbital simulation, planetary rendering, lunar terrain/worldgen, hologram tables, and built-in HTTP telemetry servers have been removed so you can use every thruster, engine, gyroscope, separator, instrument, power system, and piece of flight gear directly in your normal worlds and modpacks.

---

## Key Features

### Propulsion & Engines
* **Rocket Thruster & Vector Thruster:** Liquid-fueled thrusters supporting Lava and tagged Create/addon fuels (Kerosene, Diesel, Gasoline, etc.) with configurable min/max thrust and Redstone gimbal steering on the Vector Thruster.
* **Booster Thruster:** Solid-fuel booster fueled by Coal Blocks behind the engine — ignite with Redstone for massive single-burn thrust.
* **Creative Thruster:** Redstone-throttled thruster producing up to 10,000 N without fuel for testing and creative builds.
* **RCS Thrusters (+ Brass, Copper & Railway Encased Variants):** Precision attitude-control thrusters scaled by Redstone signal strength (up to 105 N at full signal in normal worlds).
* **Ion Engine:** Efficient Forge Energy (FE) electrical thruster (up to 180 N at full throttle).
* **Modular Thruster Mount, Engine Cycle Pipes & Nozzles:** Assemble custom liquid engines with `Copper` or `Titanium` nozzles and wrench-selectable thermodynamic cycles (`Closed`, `Open`, `Full-flow`, `Expander`).

### Ship Control, Staging & Instruments
* **Gyrodyne:** Electric Control Moment Gyroscope (CMG) stabilizer with selectable modes (`SAS`, `Attitude Hold`, `Prograde`, `Retrograde`, `Normal`, `Anti-Normal`, `Radial In`, `Radial Out`, `Horizon Level`, `Sun Tracking`) and Redstone disable support.
* **Stage Separators (Standard, Charge & Shaft):** Redstone-triggered explosive decoupling mechanisms for multi-stage contraptions.
* **Sputnik Probe & Sputnik Links:** Built-in chunk loading, visual node-graph logic editor, wireless `Sputnik Link` Redstone transceivers, Create Display Link source, and optional **ComputerCraft** peripheral support.
* **Multi-Function Flight Display (MFD) & Cartridges:** Cockpit instrument panels with attitude/FDAI, altimeter/airspeed, terrain views, and sandboxed Lua cartridge programs.

### Power, Fluids & Equipment
* **Solar Panels, Copper Wire & Energy Tanks:** Daytime solar power generation with automatic array combining, 1,000 FE/t wiring, and modular multiblock FE battery tanks (1x1, 2x2, 3x3).
* **Fluid Tank Pressure, Fuel Mass & Drain Valves:** Dynamic fluid weight on Sable ships plus high-altitude tank overpressure mechanics and Redstone-controlled Drain Valves.
* **Wearable Flight Gear:** Jetpack (6DOF flight + hover/roll controls), Copper Leg Thrusters (creative-style flight & inertial dampeners), Copper Anchor Boots, and Space Helmet.
* **Titanium Progression & Ponder:** Overworld Titanium Ore (`minecraft:overworld` underground ore generation), Titanium / Titanium Alloy processing recipes, and interactive **Ponder** scenes for engines, RCS, and Gyrodynes.

* **Create Aeronautics:** `1.0.0+`
* **Sable:** `2.0.3+`
* **Optional Integrations:** CC: Tweaked, JEI, Jade
